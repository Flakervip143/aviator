Play for fun, not for money

SkyRocket uses virtual coins and cannot pay out real money. Still, gambling-style games can encourage risky habits. Keep the demo in perspective.

No real-money gambling is offered here. You cannot deposit, withdraw, win or lose money on SkyRocket. If you want to gamble with real money, this site is not the place — and if gambling is causing harm, seek qualified support.

1. Remember what the demo is

  • All balances are virtual coins with no cash value.
  • Wins and losses are simulated and do not reflect real odds or real payouts.
  • There is no opportunity to recover money because no money was ever staked.
  • Resetting or losing local data does not cost you anything real.

2. Keep it balanced

Even without money, long sessions can affect sleep, attention and mood. Treat the demo like any other game:

  • Set a time limit before you start and use a timer.
  • Take a short break after every few rounds.
  • Stop if you feel frustrated, anxious or compelled to keep playing.
  • Do not use the demo to escape stress or to chase a “winning streak”.
  • Do not let the demo replace work, study, sleep or time with people you care about.

3. Do not spend real money

The cashier is simulated. It never charges a card, UPI ID, bank account or crypto wallet. If any page asks for real payment details, close it — it is not part of this demo. Never treat virtual coins as savings or income.

4. Age and access

The demo is for adults aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, do not use it. Parents and guardians should use device-level restrictions if gambling-style content is not appropriate for someone using the device.

5. Recognising harm

Warning signs can include:

  • Gambling or gambling-like play taking priority over responsibilities.
  • Lying about time or money spent.
  • Feeling unable to stop, or feeling distressed when unable to play.
  • Chasing losses, borrowing money or selling belongings to gamble.
  • Using gambling to cope with anxiety, depression or loneliness.

These signs apply to real gambling as well. This demo cannot treat or prevent them. If any of this sounds familiar, talk to a qualified health professional or a gambling-support service in your country.

6. Self-exclusion from this demo

SkyRocket has no server account that can be blocked remotely. To stop using it locally:

  • Use the Reset demo progress button on the account page.
  • Clear the site's data in your browser settings, or browse in a private window.
  • Use device-level website blockers or parental controls to block this domain.
  • Ask someone you trust to help you step away if needed.

7. Getting help

If gambling is affecting your life, contact a local medical professional, mental-health service or a recognised gambling-support organisation in your region. Search for “gambling support” plus your country or city, or ask a doctor for a referral. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

This demo is not a counselling service and cannot provide personalised advice.
